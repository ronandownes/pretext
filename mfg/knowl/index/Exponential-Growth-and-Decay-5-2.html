In the preceding examples, exponential growth was modeled by increasing functions of the form
\begin{equation*} P(t) = P_0 b^t \end{equation*}
where \(b \gt 1\text{.}\) The function \(P(t) = P_0b^t\) is a decreasing function if \(0 \lt b \lt 1\text{.}\) In this case, we say that the function describes exponential decay, and the constant \(b\) is called the decay factor. In Investigation 4.15, we consider two examples of exponential decay.
