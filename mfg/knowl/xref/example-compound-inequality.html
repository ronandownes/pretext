Example A.14.

Solve \(~4 \le 3x + 10 \le 16\text{.}\)
🔗

Solution.

We isolate \(x\) by performing the same operations on all three sides of the inequality.
\begin{alignat*}{3} 4 \amp{}\le{} 3x\amp {}+{}\amp 10 \amp{}\le{} 16\hphantom{blank} \amp\blert{\text{Subtract }10.}\\ -6 \amp{}\le{} \amp 3x\amp {}{}\amp {}\le{} 6\hphantom{blank} \amp\blert{\text{Divide by }3.}\\ -2 \amp{}\le{} \amp x\amp {}{}\amp{}\le{} 2\hphantom{blank}\amp \end{alignat*}
The solutions are all numbers between \(-2\) and \(2\text{,}\) inclusive.
