Example A.17.

Loren makes $\(6\) an hour working at the campus bookstore.
  1. Choose a variable for the number of hours Loren works per week.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Write an algebraic expression for the amount of Loren’s weekly earnings.
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

  1. Let \(h\) stand for the number of hours Loren works per week.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The amount Loren earns is given by
    \begin{equation*} \blert{6\times (\text{number of hours Loren worked})} \end{equation*}
    or \(6\cdot h\text{.}\) Loren’s weekly earnings can be expressed as \(6h\text{.}\)
