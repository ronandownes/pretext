Example A.80.

Write each expression as a single algebraic fraction.
  1. \(\displaystyle x^{-1}-y^{-1}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \left(x^{-2}+y^{-2}\right)^{-1}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

  1. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle{x^{-1}-y^{-1}=\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{y} ~~~\text{ or } ~~~\frac{y-x}{xy}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \displaystyle{\left(x^{-2}+y^{-2}\right)^{-1}=\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}}+\frac{1}{y^{2}}\right)^{-1} =\left(\frac{y^{2}+x^{2}}{x^2y^2}\right)^{-1}=\frac{x^2y^2}{y^2+x^2}}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗
🔗
