Exercise 44.

There are many proofs of the Pythagorean theorem. Here is a simple visual argument.
🔗
  1. What is the length of the side of the large square in the figure? Write an expression for its area.
    🔗
  2. Write another expression for the area of the large square by adding the areas of the four right triangles and the smaller central square.
    🔗
  3. Equate your two expressions for the area of the large square, and deduce the Pythagorean theorem.
    🔗
