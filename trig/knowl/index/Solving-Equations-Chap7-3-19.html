Solutions of Trigonometric Equations.

  1. The equation \(~\cos (\theta) = k~~\) for \(-1\lt k\lt 1~\) has two solutions between \(0\) and \(2\pi\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} \theta_{1}=\cos^{-1}(k)~~ \text{and}~~ \theta_{2}=2\pi -\theta_{1} \end{equation*}
    🔗
  2. The equation \(~\sin (\theta) = k~~\) for \(-1\lt k\lt 1~\) has two solutions between \(0\) and \(2\pi\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} \text{If}~k \gt 0:~~ \theta_{1} \amp =\sin^{-1}(k)~~ \text{and}~~ \theta_{2}=\pi -\theta_{1}\\ \text{If}~k \lt 0:~~ \theta_{1} \amp =\sin^{-1}(k) + 2\pi ~~ \text{and}~~ \theta_{2}=\pi -\sin^{-1}(k) \end{align*}
    🔗
  3. The equation \(~\tan (\theta) = k~\) has two solutions between \(0\) and \(2\pi\text{:}\)
    \begin{align*} \text{If}~k \gt 0:~~ \theta_{1} \amp =\tan^{-1}(k)~~ \text{and}~~ \theta_{2}=\pi +\theta_{1}\\ \text{If}~k \lt 0:~~ \theta_{1} \amp =\tan^{-1}(k) + \pi ~~ \text{and}~~ \theta_{2}=\pi +\theta_{1} \end{align*}
    🔗
