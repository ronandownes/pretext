Operations on Vectors.

  1. We can multiply a vector, \(\bf{v}\text{,}\) by a scalar, \(k\text{.}\)
    🔗
    1. If \(k \gt 0\text{,}\) the magnitude of \(k\bf{v}\) is \(k\) times the magnitude of \(\bf{v}\text{.}\) The direction of \(k\bf{v}\) is the same as the direction of \(\bf{v}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. If \(k \lt 0\text{,}\) the direction of \(k\bf{v}\) is opposite the direction of \(\bf{v}\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗
