The Discriminant.

The discriminant of a quadratic equation is
\begin{equation*} \blert{D=b^2-4ac} \end{equation*}
  1. If \(D \gt 0\text{,}\) there are two unequal real solutions.
    🔗
  2. If \(D = 0\text{,}\) there is one solution of multiplicity two.
    🔗
  3. If \(D \lt 0\text{,}\) there are two complex conjugate solutions.
    🔗
