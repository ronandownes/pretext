Point-Slope Form.

The equation of the line that passes through the point \((x_1, y_1)\) and has slope \(m\) is
\begin{equation*} \blert{y = y_1 + m(x-x_1) } \end{equation*}
You may also see the formula written in an alternate version:
\begin{equation*} \blert{y - y_1 = m(x-x_1)}\qquad\text{ or }\qquad \blert{\frac{y-y_1}{x-x_1}=m} \end{equation*}
