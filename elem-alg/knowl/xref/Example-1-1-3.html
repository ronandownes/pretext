Example 1.5.

  1. What is the value of \(h\) when \(t=85\text{?}\) What do these values mean in this situation?
    🔗
  2. When is the plane descending?
    🔗
🔗

Solution.

  1. The graph shows that \(h\) is approximately 20 when \(t=85\text{.}\) This means that at 85 minutes into the flight, the plane’s altitude is about 20,000 feet.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. The plane is descending when its altitude is decreasing. On the graph, \(h\) begins to decrease at \(t=70\) and continues decreasing until \(t=110\text{.}\) Thus, the plane is descending from the 70th minute until the end of the flight at 110 minutes.
