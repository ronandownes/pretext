Look Closer.

What about the equation of a vertical line? The slope of a vertical line is undefined; a vertical line does not have a slope. We cannot use the slope-intercept form to write the equation of a vertical line. However, we can use what we learned about horizontal lines in Example 3.78.
🔗
Look at the graph of the vertical line at right. Every point on the line has \(x\)-coordinate \(-2\text{,}\) no matter what the \(y\)-coordinate is. An equation for this line is \(x=-2\text{.}\) The value of \(x\) does not depend upon \(y\text{;}\) it is constant, so \(y\) does not appear in the equation.
