Properties of Radicals.

Product Rule for Radicals
\begin{gather*} \text{If}~~a,~b \ge 0,~~~\text{then}~~~~\blert{\sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{a}\sqrt{b}} \end{gather*}
🔗
Quotient Rule for Radicals
\begin{gather*} \text{If}~~a \ge 0,~b \gt 0~~~\text{then}~~~~\blert{\sqrt{\dfrac{a}{b}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}} \end{gather*}
