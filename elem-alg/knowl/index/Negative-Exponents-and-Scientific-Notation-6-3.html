Laws of Exponents.

  1. \begin{gather*} \blert{a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}} \end{gather*}
    🔗
  2. \begin{gather*} \blert{\dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}~~~~(a \not= 0)} \end{gather*}
    🔗
  3. \begin{gather*} \blert{\left(a^m\right)^n = a^{mn}} \end{gather*}
    🔗
  4. \begin{gather*} \blert{(ab)^n = a^nb^n}~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ \end{gather*}
    🔗
  5. \begin{gather*} \blert{\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^n = \dfrac{a^n}{b^n}} \end{gather*}
    🔗
