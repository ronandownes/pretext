Caution 8.11.

In Example 8.10, it would be incorrect to "cancel" terms of the numerator and denominator separately, as shown below:
\begin{gather*} \dfrac{\cancel{3x^2}+\bcancel{12x}}{\cancel{6x}+\bcancel{24}} \rightarrow\dfrac{x+x}{2+2}~~~~~~~\alert{\text{Incorrect!}} \end{gather*}
We must always factor the numerator and denominator before attempting to cancel.
