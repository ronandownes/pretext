In Example 5.71 we computed the product \((x-4)(x+6)\text{.}\) The top row of the rectangle corresponds to
\begin{gather*} x(x+6)=x^2+6x \end{gather*}
and the bottom row corresponds to
\begin{gather*} -4(x+6)=-4x-24 \end{gather*}
🔗
Thus, we multiply each term of the first binomial by each term of the second binomial, resulting in four multiplications in all:
\begin{gather*} (x-4)(x+6)=x^2+6x-4x-24 \end{gather*}
