Slope.

The slope of a line is defined by the ratio
\begin{gather*} \blert{\dfrac{\text{change in } y\text{-coordinate}}{\text{change in } x\text{-coordinate}}} \end{gather*}
as we move from one point to another on the line. In symbols,
\begin{gather*} \blert{m=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}} \end{gather*}
